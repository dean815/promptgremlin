## All models
- Tell the drafter to avoid the words that mark machine-written prose: "additionally" to open a sentence, delve, crucial, pivotal, intricate, tapestry, testament, underscore, showcase, foster, enhance, landscape (abstract), vibrant, robust. (signs-of-ai-writing#High density of "AI vocabulary" words)
- No "it's not X, it's Y" contrasts that correct a misconception nobody raised. (signs-of-ai-writing#Negative parallelisms)
- No reflexive groups of three (adjectives, phrases, or bolded list items) used to make a thin point look complete. (signs-of-ai-writing#Rule of three)
- No trailing "-ing" commentary on significance ("highlighting its importance", "reflecting a commitment to"); state facts and let the reader judge. (signs-of-ai-writing#Superficial analyses)
- Keep a neutral, specific tone; puffery like "boasts", "renowned", "nestled", "rich", "groundbreaking" reads as generated or promotional. (signs-of-ai-writing#Promotional and advertisement-like language)
- The draft must not talk to its requester: no "I hope this helps", "Certainly!", "let me know", or "here is a ..." inside text meant for someone else. (signs-of-ai-writing#Collaborative communication)
- These signs are strongest in combination; an edit pass should look for clusters, not ban every word on sight. (hand-written)
