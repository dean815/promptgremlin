## All models
- Higgsfield is a host for many image and video models and has no single prompt guide; the one fetched page is its Sora 2 prompt guide. Write the prompt for the underlying model the user picked, and follow that model's own notes: `sora`, `google-video` (Veo), `kling`, `nano-banana`, `gpt-image`. If the model is unknown, ask which one before writing. (hand-written: no official guide; sora-2-guide#Explore more AI features)
- Platform tags and presets are not model prose: camera moves can be typed as `#` tags (static, handheld, zoom, pan, and similar) or picked from the camera library, and they stack per shot in order. Camera bodies, lenses, and grading are presets in the UI. Put a short "UI selections" line next to the prompt listing them, rather than writing them into the text. (hand-written: research notes, not in fetched source)
- Identity and style consistency (Soul ID, Soul characters, Moodboard) are chosen in the UI, not written into the prompt. Soul-specific prompt rules are not documented; say so rather than inventing any. (hand-written)
- Choose depth on purpose: a short prompt for quick, loose results where the model fills the gaps; a structured high-control prompt when composition, timing, palette, audio, or finishing must be exact. (sora-2-guide#A prompt guide from a platform trusted by 25 million creators (FAQ))
- High-control structure: shot framing, depth of field, beat-by-beat action, lighting scheme, and color palette. Anything not stated is invented by the model. (sora-2-guide#A prompt guide from a platform trusted by 25 million creators (FAQ))
- Pair one camera move with one character action per beat, and use specific, strong verbs over vague ones. (sora-2-guide#A prompt guide from a platform trusted by 25 million creators (FAQ))
- For continuity across cuts, anchor foreground, midground, and background in the location and framing description. (sora-2-guide#A prompt guide from a platform trusted by 25 million creators (FAQ))
- The beat and depth-anchor rules come from a Sora 2 page; for other hosted models, defer to that model's notes wherever they differ. (hand-written)
