## All models
- Default to Gemini Omni Flash; use Veo for a Veo-only control: `durationSeconds` of 4/6/8, extension chains to 148s, `referenceImages` (three). Keep model and settings on a separate line. (omni#Prompt guide, veo#Veo API parameters and specifications; hand-written: choice rule)
- Omni settings: `aspect_ratio` ("16:9" default, "9:16"), `resolution` in `response_format` (`360p`, `720p` default, `1080p` and `4k` upscaled), and `task` (`image_to_video`, `edit`, `extend`, etc.), left unset unless prompting alone fails. Veo: `aspectRatio`, `durationSeconds`, `resolution`. None belong in prose. (omni#Control aspect ratio, #Output resolution, #Tasks parameter, veo#Veo API parameters and specifications)
- Describe the shot as subject, action, style, then optional camera, framing, lens or focus, and light or color mood, in descriptive language. (veo#Prompt writing basics, omni#Text to video generation)
- Omni cuts between several shots on its own. For one take, tell it the clip is a single uncut shot with no cuts between scenes. (omni#Single scene)
- Timing: plain words ("after 3 seconds...", "every 2s cut") or one timecode line per segment, like `[0-3s] ...`. (omni#Timing events)
- Audio is generated by default. State the sound wanted: dialogue in quotes, spelled-out effects, ambient noise, music mood. Write exclusions as prose ("No dialogue"); there is no negative-prompt field. (veo#Prompting for audio, omni#Prompting the audio, #Removing unwanted elements, #Limitations)
- On-screen text: quote the exact words. (omni#Text in videos)
- Edits (Omni): one short instruction per turn, plus "Keep everything else the same" when one aspect changes; long edits cause unrequested changes. Turns chain via `previous_interaction_id`. (omni#Prompts for editing, #Stateful video editing)
- Image and video roles (Omni) are bound by tags in the prompt: `<FIRST_FRAME>`, `<LAST_FRAME>` (needs a first frame), `<IMAGE_REF_N>`, `<VIDEO_REF_N>`, counting from 0. Multi-input jobs declare them first, as `[# Sources <FIRST_FRAME>@Image1]` or `[# References <IMAGE_REF_0>@Image2]`, then close by saying whether a reference is a literal frame. (omni#Using tags in prompts to set image and video roles)
- Image-to-video: name the camera move, subject motion, and environment effects, not "make it move". (omni#Image to video generation)
- Flow: `@Name` calls a saved character (`@me` = your avatar); Video Ingredients and Frames are chosen in the model menu. Describe the start-to-end transition, mention every frame or ingredient supplied, never contradict them. Subject refs need plain backgrounds; style refs, no stray subjects. `@Voice: Name` works only with ingredients on Omni Flash. (flow#Add characters, #Add start & end frames, #Add visual references, #Add voice references, #Best practices)

## gemini-omni-1-1-flash
- Flagship, generates video with audio. Extension adds 10 seconds up to 40 total, from the last 10 seconds; say whether the scene continues or cuts, and what the audio does. In timestamps, 0s is the start of the new part. (omni#Prompts for extending a video)
- Video reference: at most 3 clips of 3 seconds, likeness works best, its audio is ignored. No audio references, voice editing, multi-video prompts, or system instructions. (omni#Limitations)
- Uploaded videos for edit or extension must be 10 seconds or shorter, and are unavailable in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. (omni#Limitations)

## veo-3-1-generate-preview
- Preview. Prompt text up to 1,024 tokens. `durationSeconds` "4", "6", "8"; 8 is required with 1080p, 4k, extension, or reference images. (veo#Veo 3.1 Preview, #Veo API parameters and specifications)
- Last-frame control: pass `lastFrame` with `image`, and write the action between them. Up to three `referenceImages` of one person, character, or product. (veo#Using first and last frames, #Prompting with reference images)
- Extension: adds 7 seconds, up to 20 times, to a Veo-made 720p clip, 148 seconds total at most; it continues the final second, and speech only carries over if present in it. (veo#Extending Veo videos, #Prompting for extension)

## veo-3-1-fast-generate-preview
- Preview speed tier with the same parameters and abilities as `veo-3-1-generate-preview`, including references, `lastFrame`, extension, and 4k. Use it for quick iterations. (veo#Veo API parameters and specifications, #Model features, #Veo 3 Fast)
- Prompt text up to 1,024 tokens. (veo#Veo 3.1 Fast Preview)

## veo-3-1-lite-generate-preview
- Preview, lowest tier. Text-to-video and image-to-video with `lastFrame`; no reference images, no extension, no video input, and no 4k. 1080p needs 8 seconds. (veo#Model features, #Veo API parameters and specifications)
- Prompt text up to 1,024 tokens. (veo#Veo 3.1 Lite Preview)
